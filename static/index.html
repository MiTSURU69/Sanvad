Sanvad is a support bot for small businesses. It answers in Hindi, Hinglish or English, and only from the information you give it about your business.

रिफंड कितने दिन में मिलता है?
Hinglish

English

Try one, or type your own. The dashed question is outside what this business knows.

These are sample businesses. Each answer comes from that business's sample information, nothing else.

It would rather say "I don't know" than guess.

Every question goes through two checks. First, the question is compared with your documents, and a weak match never reaches the language model. Then the model is told to decline if the text it was given doesn't actually answer the question.

It has the answer
It replies in the language the customer wrote in, using only the text from your documents.
It doesn't have the answer
It says so plainly and logs the question, so you can see what customers ask that your documents don't cover. Alerts can also go to Telegram.
The customer wants a person
If you've given it a WhatsApp number, it adds a link to your team with the customer's question already filled in.

Each business sees only its own information.

A question is searched against one business's documents and no one else's. ShopEasy's bot can't see SmileCare's fees or LearnLoop's courses, even if a customer asks for them directly.

Automated tests check this with questions in English, Hindi and Hinglish, and they must show zero leaks.

Add it to your website with one line.

Paste this just before the closing </body> tag of your page and replace the business id with yours. A chat button appears at the bottom right.
