## Course catalog

LearnLoop offers four courses: Python Programming (8 weeks), Data Analytics with Excel and SQL (10 weeks), Web Development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript (12 weeks), and Spoken English for Professionals (6 weeks). All courses are online and self-paced, with a live doubt-clearing session every week.

## Python Programming course

## Certificates

Yes. You receive a completion certificate after finishing all modules and scoring at least 60 percent on the final quiz. It appears in your dashboard as a downloadable PDF within 48 hours.
