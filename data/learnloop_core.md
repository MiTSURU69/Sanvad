## Course access
Questions: How do I access my course? / Where can I watch my classes? / course kaise dekhu / meri classes kahan milengi / कोर्स कैसे देखें?
Log in to the LearnLoop app or website with your registered email. All purchased courses appear under My Learning, with lifetime access.

## Refunds
Questions: Can I get a refund? / What is the refund policy? / refund milega kya / course ka paisa wapas kaise milega / रिफंड कैसे मिलेगा?
You can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have completed less than 20 percent of the course. Refunds reach your original payment method in 5-7 business days.

## Certificates
Questions: Will I get a certificate? / How do I download my certificate? / certificate kaise milega / certificate kab milta hai / सर्टिफिकेट कैसे मिलेगा?
A certificate is issued automatically when you complete all lessons and pass the final quiz. Download it from My Learning, under Certificates.

## Doubts
Questions: How can I ask a doubt? / Is there a mentor for support? / doubt kaise puchu / mentor se baat kaise karein / संदेह कैसे पूछें?
Post your question in the Doubts tab under any lesson. A mentor replies within 24 hours on weekdays.
