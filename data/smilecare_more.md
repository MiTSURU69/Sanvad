## Treatments and prices
Questions: What treatments do you offer? / How much does a root canal cost? / dant ka ilaaj kitne ka hai? / scaling ki fees kya hai?
SmileCare offers general check-ups (Rs 500), scaling and polishing (Rs 1,500), fillings (from Rs 1,200), root canal treatment (from Rs 6,000), braces consultation (Rs 800) and crowns (from Rs 5,000). Final prices depend on the dentist's examination.

## Teeth whitening
Questions: Do you offer teeth whitening? / teeth whitening ka kharcha kitna hai? / can I whiten my teeth?
Yes. Professional teeth whitening takes about 60 minutes in one visit and costs Rs 7,500. Results usually last 6 to 12 months. A short check-up beforehand confirms it is suitable for you.

## Insurance and payment
Questions: Do you accept insurance? / Can I pay by UPI or card? / kya aap insurance lete hain? / payment kaise kar sakte hain?
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and cash. Dental insurance claims are supported for major providers. Please bring your insurance card to the first visit.

## After a procedure
Questions: What should I do after a tooth extraction? / what can I eat after a filling? / ilaaj ke baad kya khana chahiye?
After a procedure, avoid hot food and drinks for 24 hours, do not rinse hard on the first day, and take any prescribed medicine on time. Mild soreness is normal.

## Dental emergencies
Questions: I have severe tooth pain, what should I do? / tooth pain bahut ho raha hai / I broke my tooth
For severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth, call our front desk as soon as possible and mention it is an emergency so we can fit you in the same day.
