## Shipping
Questions: How long does delivery take? / Is there free shipping? / Is express delivery available? / order kitne din me aayega / delivery mein kitna time lagega / free shipping milti hai kya / delivery charge kitna hai / ऑर्डर कितने दिन में आएगा? / क्या फ्री शिपिंग मिलती है?
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Orders above Rs. 999 get free shipping. Express delivery (1-2 days) costs Rs. 99.
