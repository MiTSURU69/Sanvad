## Warranty
Questions: Do products come with a warranty? / warranty kitne saal ki hoti hai? / how do I claim warranty?
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Keep your invoice and contact support with your order number to start a warranty claim.

## Coupons and offers
Questions: Do you have any discount codes? / coupon code kaise lagate hain? / how do I apply a promo code?
Enter your coupon code in the box on the cart page before paying. Only one code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied after the order is placed.

## Cancelling an order
Questions: How do I cancel my order? / order cancel kaise karun? / can I cancel after payment?
You can cancel from the My Orders page until the order is dispatched. After dispatch, refuse the delivery or start a return once it arrives.

## Out of stock products
Questions: When will an out of stock item be available? / product stock mein kab aayega? / can I get notified when it is back?
Use the Notify Me button on the product page and we will email you when it is back in stock.
