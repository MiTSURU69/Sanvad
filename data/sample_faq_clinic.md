## Appointments
Questions: How do I book an appointment? / Can I book online? / appointment kaise book karein / doctor se milne ka time kaise milega / अपॉइंटमेंट कैसे बुक करें?
You can book an appointment on our website or by calling the front desk between 9 AM and 7 PM, Monday to Saturday. Walk-ins are accepted subject to doctor availability.

## Timings
Questions: What are your clinic timings? / Is the clinic open on Sunday? / clinic kab khulta hai / Sunday ko open hai kya / क्लिनिक का समय क्या है?
The clinic is open from 9 AM to 7 PM, Monday to Saturday. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Consultation fees
Questions: How much is the consultation fee? / What does a doctor visit cost? / consultation fees kitni hai / doctor ki fees kitni lagegi / परामर्श शुल्क कितना है?
The general physician consultation fee is Rs. 500. Specialist consultations cost Rs. 800. A follow-up visit within 7 days is free.

## Reports
Questions: How do I get my test reports? / Where can I download my report? / report kab milegi / meri report kaise milegi / रिपोर्ट कब मिलेगी?
Test reports are ready in 24-48 hours and are sent to your registered mobile number by SMS. You can also collect a printed copy from the front desk.
